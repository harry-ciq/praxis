# Mission

Praxis exists because LinkedIn rewards self-promotion over actual output. Your resume says "10x engineer" but your GitHub says 3 commits last quarter. We think professionals deserve a network that knows the difference.

## What We Do

Praxis is an execution-driven professional network. We connect to the platforms where real work happens — GitHub, YouTube, and more — and surface verified achievements automatically. No one writes "Thrilled to announce..." on Praxis. The platform announces it for you, with receipts.

Once a week, an AI digest summarises everything that happened — yours and the people you follow — into a one-page issue: a hero stat, a vibe, a pull-quote highlight, near-miss milestones to chase, and a forward-looking "what to watch". Every line links back to the underlying achievement. It's the recap you'd actually read.

## Who We Serve

- **Developers & Creators** — people who ship code, publish content, and build things. Their work speaks for itself; Praxis gives it a microphone.
- **Hiring Teams** — tired of resume fiction. They want to see verified commit streaks, open-source contributions, and real project history before scheduling a call.
- **Companies** — posting jobs that match on proven skills, not keyword-stuffed bullet points.

## What Makes Us Different

On LinkedIn, achievements are self-reported. On Praxis, achievements are platform-verified. You can't claim a 30-day commit streak you didn't earn. You can't fake an open-source contribution. The feed shows what people actually did, not what they say they did.

## What Success Looks Like

A professional network where your profile builds itself from real work. Where following someone means seeing their actual output, not their thought leadership. Where a job listing says "requires `STARS_MILESTONE:100`" and the platform can confirm it in milliseconds — and the candidate sees a green check next to that requirement on the job card.
